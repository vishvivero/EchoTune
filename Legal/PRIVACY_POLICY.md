# Privacy Policy - EchoTune

**Last Updated:** November 2, 2025
**Effective Date:** November 2, 2025

## Introduction

Welcome to EchoTune ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

**Short Version:** EchoTune processes your voice locally on your Mac. We don't collect, store, or transmit your transcriptions. Your privacy is our priority.

---

## Information We Collect

### 1. Audio Data (Voice Recordings)

**What we collect:** Your voice when you use the recording feature.

**How we use it:**
- To convert your speech to text (transcription)
- Processed locally on your device (when using local AI models)
- Sent to third-party providers only if you choose cloud models

**Retention:**
- Audio is **NOT stored** anywhere
- Immediately discarded after transcription
- No recordings are saved to disk

**Your control:**
- You initiate every recording (⌘⇧D shortcut)
- Recording indicator always visible
- You can stop anytime

### 2. Usage Statistics (Local Only)

**What we collect:**
- Number of transcriptions performed
- Total words transcribed
- Recording duration
- Keyboard shortcut usage
- Feature usage (which AI models you use)

**Storage:** Stored locally on your Mac only (UserDefaults)

**Purpose:**
- Show you personal statistics
- Help you track your productivity
- No analytics sent to us

**Your control:** You can clear statistics anytime in Settings

### 3. Device Information

**What we collect:**
- Hardware UUID (for license activation only)
- macOS version (for compatibility)
- App version

**Purpose:**
- License validation (one license per device)
- Ensure app compatibility
- Technical support

**Not collected:**
- No advertising IDs
- No tracking identifiers
- No location data

### 4. License Information

**What we store:**
- License key (in macOS Keychain, encrypted)
- Activation date
- Device fingerprint (hardware UUID)

**Purpose:**
- Verify your purchase
- Prevent license abuse
- Manage device activations

**Storage:** Securely stored in macOS Keychain

## How We Use Your Information

### Local Processing (Default)

When using **local AI models** (Whisper):
- ✅ Everything happens on your Mac
- ✅ No internet connection required
- ✅ No data leaves your device
- ✅ 100% private

### Cloud Processing (Optional)

When you **choose** to use **cloud AI models** (Groq, Deepgram):
- ⚠️ Audio sent to third-party provider
- ⚠️ Processed on their servers
- ⚠️ Subject to their privacy policies
- ✅ EchoTune does NOT store your audio
- ✅ We are just a client passing data

**Cloud Providers:**
- **Groq:** See [Groq Privacy Policy](https://groq.com/privacy)
- **Deepgram:** See [Deepgram Privacy Policy](https://deepgram.com/privacy)

**You control:** You provide your own API keys, choose when to use cloud models

## Data Storage and Security

### What We Store Locally

**On your Mac:**
- App preferences and settings
- Usage statistics (word count, session count)
- License information (encrypted in Keychain)
- AI model files you download

**NOT stored:**
- Your voice recordings
- Your transcribed text (inserted into apps immediately)
- Personal information
- Browsing history

### Security Measures

- **Keychain Storage:** Sensitive data encrypted by macOS
- **No Cloud Sync:** Nothing synced to our servers
- **Local Processing:** Most data never leaves your Mac
- **Sandboxed:** App runs in macOS sandbox for security

## Your Rights and Choices

### What You Can Do

**Access Your Data:**
- All data is stored locally on your Mac
- View statistics in Settings → Statistics
- View license info in Settings → License

**Delete Your Data:**
- Clear statistics: Settings → Privacy → Clear History
- Remove license: Settings → License → Deactivate
- Uninstall app: All data removed with app

**Control Cloud Usage:**
- Use local models only (100% private)
- Choose cloud models selectively
- Provide/revoke API keys anytime
- Delete API keys: Settings → AI Models → Disconnect

**Opt-Out of Everything:**
- Don't use cloud models = No data leaves your device
- Clear statistics = No usage tracking
- Local processing only = Complete privacy
