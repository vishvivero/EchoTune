# EchoTune

**Transform Your Voice into Text on macOS**

EchoTune is a privacy-first, AI-powered voice dictation application for macOS that enables you to convert speech to text anywhere on your Mac using state-of-the-art local AI models. Unlike cloud-based solutions, EchoTune processes all audio locally on your device, ensuring complete privacy and offline functionality.

---

## 🎯 What is EchoTune?

EchoTune is a system-wide voice-to-text application that works seamlessly across all macOS applications. Simply press a keyboard shortcut (default: ⌘⇧D), speak naturally, and watch your words appear instantly in any app—whether you're writing emails, coding, taking notes, or chatting with colleagues.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔒 Privacy-First Design
- **100% Local Processing**: All voice data is processed on your device by default
- **No Data Transmission**: Your audio never leaves your Mac unless you explicitly choose cloud models
- **Offline Capable**: Works completely offline with local AI models
- **No Storage**: Audio is never saved or stored on your device

### 🚀 System-Wide Integration
- **Works Everywhere**: Dictate in any macOS application—email clients, code editors, browsers, chat apps, and more
- **Global Keyboard Shortcut**: Customizable shortcut (default ⌘⇧D) works system-wide
- **Automatic Text Insertion**: Text appears directly where you're typing—no copy-pasting needed
- **Menu Bar Access**: Quick access to controls and recent transcriptions from the menu bar

### 🤖 Multiple AI Models
Choose the model that fits your needs:

- **Fast Model** (27MB): 5-10x realtime speed, good accuracy
- **Balanced Model** (140MB): 2-3x realtime speed, very good accuracy  
- **Accurate Model** (550MB): 1x realtime speed, excellent accuracy
- **Cloud Models**: Optional Groq, Deepgram, and other cloud providers for ultra-fast processing

### 🌍 Language Support
- **99+ Languages**: Supports transcription in nearly any language
- **Automatic Language Detection**: Intelligently detects the language you're speaking
- **Accent Handling**: Works well with various accents and dialects
- **Technical Terms**: Handles technical terminology and proper nouns accurately

### 📊 Productivity Features
- **Statistics Dashboard**: Track your productivity metrics
  - Total words transcribed
  - Time saved vs. typing
  - Usage streaks
  - Speaking speed (WPM)
- **Transcription History**: Save and search past transcriptions
- **Dictionary Management**: Custom word dictionary for specialized terms
- **Notes Organization**: Keep and organize your transcriptions

### 🎨 User Experience
- **Beautiful UI**: Modern, native macOS design with dark mode support
- **Real-time Feedback**: Visual recording indicator and audio waveform
- **7-Step Onboarding**: Guided setup process for first-time users
- **Comprehensive Settings**: Fine-tune every aspect of your dictation experience

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Installation
1. Download EchoTune from the [official website](https://echotune.app) or Mac App Store
2. Drag to your Applications folder
3. Launch EchoTune

### 2. Onboarding
EchoTune includes a comprehensive 7-step onboarding process:
1. **Welcome**: Interactive introduction with animated text
2. **Microphone Permission**: Grant microphone access
3. **Microphone Selection**: Choose your preferred input device
4. **Accessibility Permission**: Enable system-wide text insertion
5. **Keyboard Shortcut Setup**: Configure your activation shortcut (default: Function key)
6. **AI Model Download**: Download recommended transcription model
7. **Try It Out**: Test your setup with a live dictation session

### 3. Start Dictating
1. Press your keyboard shortcut (default ⌘⇧D or your configured function key)
2. Speak clearly into your microphone
3. Release the shortcut to process and insert text
4. Your transcribed text appears instantly!
