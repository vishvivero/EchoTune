FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Is my audio really private?

Yes. By default, all transcription happens locally on your Mac using the Whisper AI model. Your audio never touches the internet. If you choose to use cloud models (Groq/Deepgram), you control when and use your own API keys.

What languages are supported?

EchoTune supports 99+ languages including English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Chinese (Mandarin & Cantonese), Korean, Arabic, Hindi, and many more.

Does it work offline?

Yes! The local Whisper models work completely offline. No internet required. Cloud models (optional) require internet connectivity.

What are the system requirements?

macOS 13.0 (Ventura) or later. Works on both Intel and Apple Silicon Macs. Apple Silicon recommended for best performance.

How accurate is the transcription?

Whisper AI achieves near-human accuracy for clear speech. The larger models (Base, Medium) provide even better accuracy for complex audio, accents, and technical terminology.

Can I use it in any app?

Yes! EchoTune uses macOS Accessibility APIs to insert text directly at your cursor in virtually any application—Mail, Slack, VS Code, Notes, browsers, you name it.
